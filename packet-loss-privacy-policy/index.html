English

Packet Loss is a mobile game developed by Cemoli Studio.

Packet Loss does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data.

The game does not require an internet connection to play and does not use a backend server.

Local Data

Packet Loss may store gameplay preferences locally on your device, such as:

  • Local high score.
  • Tutorial progress.
  • Audio, haptics, and reduced flash settings.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to Cemoli Studio or to third parties.

Services Not Used

Packet Loss does not use:

  • Advertising SDKs.
  • Analytics SDKs.
  • Account login.
  • Cloud saves.
  • In-app purchases.
  • External crash reporting.
  • Location tracking.
  • Push notifications.

Changes

If this changes in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated before the feature is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ml.arnulfo.c00@gmail.com.
